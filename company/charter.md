# Demo Co: charter

> Founder-owned. Sandbox company whose only purpose is to prove that the Company OS runs end to end (launch-company skill). Workers read it and never edit it.

- **Stage:** launch
- **Last approved:** 2026-11-04 by Carlos (setup by Claude)

## Mission
Prove, with real runs, that a company can be operated by its Company OS: schedules, agenda, gates, alarms, workers, landing.

## Customer and problem
The founder, who needs evidence that the operating system works before trusting it with real businesses.

## North star and targets
| Metric | Definition | Now | Target | By |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clean worker runs | Runs that land `ok` with no alarm | 0 | 3 | 2026-11-05 |

## Non-negotiables
- No spend. No changes to the existing ML template code in `src/`, `data/`, `models/`, `notebooks/`.
- This is a sandbox: workers keep their output small.

## Stage exit criteria
None: this company stays a sandbox.
