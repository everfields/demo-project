# Playbook: B2B software

Checked 2026-11-06. [F] is a formula, [R] a rule of thumb. Mind the outreach rules first: `../rules-of-the-road.md`. Promotional cold email is not a free channel for a company in Spain.

**`market.unit`:** paying customers with no tie to the founder. **Target:** 10 to 20, not 100. Most founders felt product-market fit at [3–10 paying customers](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/finding-product-market-fit).

## The route

| Stage | The question | What happens | Bar to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| idea | Does the problem hurt, and who pays? | Conversations with people who have it: past behaviour, no pitch. List 100 named buyers | ~30 conversations ([the median](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-validate-your-b2b-startup)) with a repeated, specific pain |
| validation | Will they commit? | Design partners and pre-sales | Commitments from strangers: paid pilots, letters, deposits |
| mvp | Can we deliver for them? | Build for the partners, by hand where you can | Continued use of a rough version; a partner renews or pays list price |
| launch | Can we sell without warm intros? | One repeatable motion | [~10 % first-call-to-close without warm intros](https://www.firstround.com/levels), and the target |

## Finding the first ten
- [Network first](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-win-your-first-10-b2b-customers), then targeted outbound, investor networks, communities, content, press. Trust sets the order. At the start, 1 in 10–20 warm intros converts.
- A person does the outreach: the founder or a helper, one to one. LinkedIn [bans bots](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement) for messages and invites, and in Spain promotional email needs consent, business addresses included. The worker prepares the list of public profiles and the reason to write to each; a person decides and sends.
- Cadence examples: [Okta 15–18 new contacts a month, Sprig 5 meetings a week](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-win-your-first-10-b2b-customers).
- **Inbound that needs no permission:** a free tool, a calculator or a benchmark report for the buyer's exact problem, listed where they search.

## Design partners and pre-sales
[About a third of founders](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-validate-your-b2b-startup) used them. [Sierra](https://review.firstround.com/sierra-design-partnership/): paid upfront, 10–20 % of contract value, a box of 2–6 months, weekly feedback; about 40 targets gave 6 paying partners. Absence isn't fatal: Amplitude pre-sold nothing.

## The funnel
- Activation: [SaaS median 30 %](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-a-good-activation-rate).
- [Freemium to paid 3–5 % good; trial to paid 8–12 % good](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-a-good-free-to-paid-conversion).
- [6-month retention, SMB 60/80](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-good-retention-issue-29), good/great.
- Where outreach is lawful [F]: messages needed = meetings ÷ (reply rate × positive share × booking rate). Reply rate measures [message fit](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/finding-product-market-fit). Small batches of 21–50 got [6.2 % against 2.4 % at 500+](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email).

## Expect it to take time
Idea to fit took a [median of about 2 years](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/finding-product-market-fit) among 24 top companies (survivor bias), and ~40 % pivoted. Patience on the problem, not on the solution.

## Decisions along the way
- **Price:** a price point inside a test is free. A list price is a strategy gate.
- **Build or stay manual:** manual until a paying customer's use breaks it.
- **Security and contracts:** anything that binds the company legally is a strategy gate.
