# Playbook: productized service

One defined outcome at one price, delivered by agents with as little human time as possible. Rebates is this founder's case: six weeks without a closed job, because nobody outside was asked. Checked 2026-11-06. No rigorous benchmark exists, so this is a procedure. [F] is a formula.

**`market.unit`:** paying clients with no tie to the founder. **Target:** 3–5 first, inferred from First Round's [earliest level](https://www.firstround.com/levels), then 100.

## The route

| Stage | The question | What happens | Bar to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| idea | Will anyone hand this over? | A [one-page spec](https://manyrequests.com/blog/productized-consulting): what they get, when, revision limits, exclusions | Strangers with the problem ask how to start |
| validation | Will they pay before delivery? | A [deposit](https://mtlynch.io/book-reports/the-mom-test/), the strongest commitment. Do the first jobs by hand | 3–5 non-friends paying list price |
| mvp | Can we deliver without the founder? | The workflow tested on a real job before the next client signs | Delivery time per client falling, quality holding |
| launch | Can we fill the queue? | One channel | The target, at a margin the formula accepts |

## The arithmetic [F]
- **Margin** = (price − agent cost − human minutes × rate) ÷ price. Watch delivery time per client fall.
- **First 30 days:** gross profit above [acquisition cost plus cost of goods](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained).

## Rules for this pattern
- **Run a real job before anything else.** Rebates' test runs ended in false closes from worker bugs, and no real negotiation had finished. A real job with a real person finds that in a day.
- **A test that needs three friends needs three names and a date.** Warm tests get signed and skipped. Give it an owner.
- **If the service acts for a client, the client knows an AI is doing it,** and whoever it contacts is told too. See `../rules-of-the-road.md`.
- **Success-fee models:** a first win is the only proof. Don't scale before one.
- **Regulated work** (legal, financial, medical): a strategy gate before the first client.

## Where the first clients come from
Warm first, through a person. Then the places they ask for help, also through a person, and free tools that show the result before asking for anything.
