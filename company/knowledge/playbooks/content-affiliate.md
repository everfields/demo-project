# Playbook: content and affiliate sites

The pattern of IntelligencePick and RoomRacer. Checked 2026-11-06. [F] is a formula. There is no universal traffic bar, so this playbook uses gates and a formula.

**`market.unit`:** qualifying sales, not visits. **Target:** the program's first gate, then 100.

## Do the arithmetic first [F]
Revenue per 1,000 sessions = 1,000 × click-out rate × purchase rate × order value × commission.

Our record: 2024, about 400 visits a month, 125 Amazon clicks, 13 % conversion, about $11 a month. Amazon pays about $20 on a $2,000 rig, brand programs $100–150. [Amazon cut commissions by up to 50 %](https://www.adweek.com/media/amazon-associates-affiliate-rate-cuts-publishers/) in 2026. If the model needs traffic you can't reach, change the model before you build.

## The route

| Stage | The question | What happens | Bar to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| idea | Who buys through us, and why us? | One narrow topic and a real reason to trust the site | Real people from the topic find it useful |
| validation | Will readers click and buy? | A few first-hand pages sent to real people, not waiting for Google | Click-outs and first sales from humans |
| launch | Can we reach buyers repeatably? | Search, a community, a newsletter, a brand program | Amazon's [3 qualifying sales in 180 days](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM), then [10 in 30 days](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/introduction) for the Creators API |
| growth | Does it compound? | The growth review owns it | Sales per 1,000 sessions, by page type |

Rejected Amazon accounts [aren't reinstated](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM), so don't apply before the site can reach the first gate.

## What gets a site killed
- **Scale from a template on a young domain.** 2024: ~96,000 URLs, then 20 to 2 users a day. 2026: 720 pages, demoted within a week. [Scaled content abuse applies however pages are made, and thin affiliation is a separate violation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies). The [August 2026 spam update](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/) hit an Amazon affiliate site built on AI text, and such hits can take whole domains.
- **No first-hand evidence.** [Review guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews) asks for evidence, measurements, comparisons and multiple sellers.
- **Amazon's terms.** [Original content required; Program Content can't be used to develop LLMs](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies); agents must identify themselves. Any pipeline that feeds Amazon data to a model needs review.

## Lead indicators
Search lags: [1.74–6.11 % of new pages reach the top 10 within a year](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank/). So lead with non-brand impressions, click-out rate and earnings per click, and exclude bots: our early traffic was mostly bots, and crawling is not demand. AI Overviews cut clicks at position 1 by [an estimated 58 %](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/).

## Channels beyond search
- Send the best pages to the people who need them, in the places they gather: a person, through the human pack.
- Brand programs pay more and sometimes want proof of traffic. [Networks refuse sites without traffic](../market-learnings.md); go direct where a brand has a reason to say yes.
- A report or dataset people cite earns links without outreach, but a launch that waits for one asset misses its window.
- Assistants and MCP directories are cheap hygiene, not a first-customers channel.
