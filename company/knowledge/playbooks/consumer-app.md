# Playbook: consumer app or web product

Checked 2026-11-06. [F] is a formula. AntiSearch and Bot for President are this founder's own record here: output and automated posting did not make an audience.

**`market.unit`:** people active twice in two weeks. **Target:** 100. Sign-ups don't count.

## The route

| Stage | The question | What happens | Bar to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| idea | Does anyone want this? | A narrow group you can reach by hand, and a promise tested on them | Strangers from the group try it, and some come back |
| validation | Do they return? | [50–100 friends-of-friends](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/growth-ama-with-yc-partner-gustaf-alstromer) for repeat use, before any ads | A return rate you set before the test |
| mvp | Does it deliver? | The core loop only | 40 %+ "very disappointed", [Ellis's test](https://review.firstround.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/), from about 40 answers among active users |
| launch | Can we reach 100 repeatably? | One channel | The target, and [5–7 % weekly growth](https://paulgraham.com/growth.html) |

## Where the first users came from
Of 100+ consumer companies: [places the audience gathers 50 %+, targeted strangers 30 %, press 20 %+, friends 20 %, viral content 10 %, physical placement 10 %](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/consumer-business-find-first-users). Most of those places are human-only (`../rules-of-the-road.md`), so this playbook leans on the human pack. Start with a group small enough to reach [critical mass](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html).

## Lessons from our own attempts
- Don't start an empty account and post into it. About 180 clips made 8 followers; 33 X posts made 2.
- Borrow an audience: someone who already has these people's attention.
- A launch spike isn't validation: [400+ Product Hunt sign-ups, one payer](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/400-signups-from-product-hunt-1-paying-customer-what-4-days-taught-me-about-launch-vs-traction-7c0aacf745).
- Short video: small accounts reach far more on [YouTube Shorts](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-reels-vs-youtube-shorts/) than on Reels or TikTok. Only with a real audience behind the plan.

## The numbers
- [6-month retention, good/great](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-kickstart-and-scale-a-consumer-9c8): social 25/45, transactional 30/50, subscription 40/70.
- [Activation](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-a-good-activation-rate): median 25 %.
- Apps ([RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)): download to paid by day 35, median 2.0 %; revenue per install $0.23 at day 14; AI apps churn 30 % faster.
- [F] Maximum paid cost per install = revenue per install at your payback day. With a median of $0.23 at day 14, few paid installs pay back fast, so paid acquisition usually waits for retention.

## Decisions along the way
- **Web or app store:** web first. A store adds review, accounts and fees, so it waits for a return signal.
- **Paid:** only after retention exists. Ads filling a leaking bucket tell you nothing.
- **Sensitive data or minors:** a strategy gate before launch.
