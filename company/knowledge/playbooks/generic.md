# Playbook: generic

For a business that matches no other playbook. The route is the same for every company; the numbers are yours to set before the data.

## What counts
Decide `market.unit` and `market.target` first: the smallest act that proves real value. Users who came back, paying customers, orders, signed letters of intent. A sign-up alone is the weakest of these. A high-value B2B company may set 10 or 20, not 100.

## The route

| Stage | The question | Cheapest real test | Bar to move on (set yours before the data) |
|---|---|---|---|
| idea | Does anyone care? | The offer in one sentence, put to people who have the problem: conversations, replies in the places they gather, a page | Strangers from the target answer and describe the problem in their own words, without being led |
| validation | Will they act? | An action that costs them something: a sign-up with their real address, a deposit, a pre-order, a manual version they pay for | A bar written before the test: how many, out of how many reached, in how long |
| mvp | Can we deliver the promise? | The first customers served by hand wherever you can, and software only where hand work breaks | The first customers reach the core value and come back, or say they would be very disappointed to lose it |
| launch | Can we reach the first customers repeatably? | Three channels tested, the weight on the one that works | The target reached, and one channel with a known cost or effort per customer |
| growth, scale | Does it grow at a known cost? | The growth review owns it from here | The charter's targets |

## Arithmetic to do on day one
- What is one customer worth, in money, over their life? If you don't know, estimate it, and say it is an estimate.
- So what may you pay, in money or in hours, to win one?
- How many people must you reach to win one, at the rates you have measured so far? That tells you how many actions the target takes, and whether the channel can ever pay.

## Decisions along the way
- **Whether to build at all:** only when a person is waiting for it.
- **Money:** free moves first, then one envelope for one question.
- **Stop or pivot:** the founder's call, through a strategy gate, with the numbers and a recommendation.

## When a pattern repeats
If this company looks like one you have seen before, say so under **Upstream** in the learnings file. On the second company of a kind, the weekly improve run gives the pattern its own playbook.
