# The first customers: the method

Checked 2026-11-06. Numbers carry their source; [R] marks a practitioner's rule of thumb, [F] a formula. Re-check anything older than a month before relying on it.

## Why this comes first
Software is cheap now. Bot for President made 1,364 commits in 15 days and reached 0 of its 3 founders. In the latest count of 385 startup shutdowns with known causes, the top root cause after running out of money was poor product-market fit, at 43 % ([CB Insights, 2026-03](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/startup-failure-reasons-top/)). Nobody measures "distribution" directly, and "no market found" is the closest measured cause. In Spain, 23 % of new businesses are gone after a year and 58 % after five ([INE](https://ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/es/DAE2023.htm)). Everything here exists to find out, early and cheaply, whether a market is there, and then to reach it.

## Seven rules
1. **Talk to people, not at them.** One reply from a real customer outweighs a thousand impressions. Our own record: about 180 clips and 8 followers; 17 friends paying 20 €.
2. **Go where they already are.** Places the audience already gathers gave 50 %+ of the first users of 100+ consumer companies, targeted strangers 30 %, press 20 %+, friends 20 % ([Lenny, 2022](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/consumer-business-find-first-users); categories overlap). Almost all growth came from one or two channels, and [nobody won with more than three](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-the-biggest-consumer-apps-got). In B2B almost everyone combined their personal network with going where customers spend time, and press [rarely started anything](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-todays-fastest-growing-b2b-businesses).
3. **Offer before product.** Test whether anyone acts before building what they would act on.
4. **One bet at a time.** One offer to one audience, with a bar written before the data.
5. **Do things that don't scale.** [Recruit by hand, start with a narrow group, set users up yourself](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html). Scale is a later problem.
6. **Count only what strangers do.** Opinions, likes and surveys [don't count](https://www.productcompass.pm/p/how-to-build-the-right-product-with). Friends are a start, not proof: payment from people with no tie to the founders is the [strongest signal](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-validate-your-b2b-startup).
7. **Answer everyone within a day.** Bot for President called this "the one that failed first".

## Who can act where: the hands
This decides what is possible. The best first channels are the ones a bot may not touch. `rules-of-the-road.md` has the detail and the sources.

| Hands | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **The worker alone** (code, official APIs) | The company's site and pages. Posts through official APIs: Bluesky, Mastodon, Instagram (professional account), LinkedIn (member posts only), X (pay per use, labelled Automated). Mail to people who opted in. Shopify through its Admin API. |
| **The COO** (browser, forms) | Directories, marketplace listings, sign-ups, dashboards, ad platforms set up as paused drafts. Its own email stays a draft. It never posts on a social network through a browser. |
| **Paid ads** | The one way to reach strangers that is built for automation: official APIs, inside an approved envelope. For a company with no audience, often the fastest honest answer. |
| **A person** (the founder, or a helper paid through a spend gate) | Reddit, Hacker News, Product Hunt, Facebook groups, LinkedIn engagement, direct messages, first-contact email where the law allows it, phone calls. On Hacker News and Product Hunt the person also writes the text. |

Cold promotional email and DMs are not a free channel for this founder: Spain's LSSI art. 21 bans them without consent, business addresses included. See the rules before any first contact.

## The order of moves
Take the first move that can work today. Closer to one real person beats wider.
1. **Warm:** the founder's circle and past users. A founder step: who, the exact message, the link. In B2B, 1 warm intro in 10–20 becomes a customer at the [earliest stage](https://www.firstround.com/levels).
2. **People who opted in or wrote to us:** answer them, follow up.
3. **Where they gather:** API channels by the worker; human-only ones in the human pack.
4. **Where buyers already search:** marketplaces, directories, comparison sites, through the COO.
5. **A paid test** inside an envelope.
6. **Borrowed audiences:** a paid placement in a newsletter, a marketplace's creator or affiliate programme, a partner. On TikTok Shop in Europe affiliates were an estimated [~70 % of revenue](https://blog.lengow.com/tiktok-shop-europe-q2-2026-e500m-across-four-markets).
7. **Useful things people find by themselves:** a free tool, a real comparison, an answer to a real question. Slow, and never at scale from a template: that is how IntelligencePick was demoted twice.
8. **Broadcast:** only behind the moves above. It has never worked here without an audience.

Pick channels the bullseye way: [one idea for each channel, rank them, test three cheaply, focus on one](https://leananalyticsbook.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/Traction-1-3.pdf). Each test answers three questions: what a customer costs, how many are there, and whether they are the right ones. Budget at most [about $1,000 and a month](https://medium.com/@yegg/the-bullseye-framework-for-getting-traction-ef49d05bfd7e) per test.

## Talking to people
From [The Mom Test](https://mtlynch.io/book-reports/the-mom-test/): ask about past behaviour, never pitch or ask hypotheticals, and treat compliments as noise. End with a commitment of [time, reputation or money](https://www.unusual.vc/rob-fitzpatricks-mom-test/). If ten people say different things, the segment is too wide. Founders trusted an idea after a [median of 30 conversations](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-validate-your-b2b-startup) (Zip ~75, Ramp 100+).

## An honest test
1. Pre-register "at least X % of Y will Z" ([Savoia](https://miro.com/miroverse/the-right-it-canvas/)), with the sample and the stop rule. [No peeking](https://www.evanmiller.org/how-not-to-run-an-ab-test.html).
2. Rank intent: click, email, call, deposit. The further along, the stronger.
3. A page, a pricing click, then "not available yet: join the list" ([Buffer's pattern](https://buffer.com/resources/idea-to-paying-customers-in-7-weeks-how-we-did-it/)), said right after the click and before any card data. Never a "Buy" button on what you can't supply: in the EU that is on the blacklist.
4. Never take money without a confirmed supplier and a stated date.
5. The bar comes from the economics [F]: minimum sign-up rate = cost per click ÷ the most you can pay for a qualified lead. The median landing page converts [6.6 %](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/) across all industries, which is context, not a target.
6. A pass shows interest, not that they will stay.

## Dead channel, or badly run?
Check in this order, and judge only the first step that fails.

| Step | Check | Failure means |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Bounces under 2 %, spam under 0.3 %, pages indexed, the post was not removed, ads' CPM sane | A bug, no verdict |
| Attention | Click rate, reply rate | The message or the creative |
| Intent | Good clicks but no add-to-cart or positive reply | The offer, the page or the product |
| Conversion | Carts or interest, no sales | Fees, checkout, trust |
| Fit | Are they the right customers? | The audience |

**How much before you may kill it** [F]: zero events in *n* tries puts the true rate below about 3/*n*, so *n* = 3 ÷ the lowest rate that would still pay ([rule of three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_three_(statistics))).
- A landing page that needs 5 %: 60 targeted visits. Needs 2 %: 150.
- Outreach that needs 1 % positive replies: 300 delivered.
- Paid: spend 3 × break-even CPA with zero sales.
- Try several distinct messages first: [four attempts](https://margin-notes-600.pages.dev/books/100m-leads/ch-12) or three angles [R]. Before 100 users, test only big swings: 2 % to 3 % needs about 3,100 per arm.
- SEO and communities have no sourced minimum. Set one in the plan, and date it.

## When it fails: the ladder
Change one thing and go again: message, offer, audience, channel. Then the strategist's stage meeting, and a go, pivot or stop call for the founder. Add volume [before optimising](https://margin-notes-600.pages.dev/books/100m-leads/ch-12) [R]: most "failures" are too few real attempts.

## Is it working?
- **Return usage, not sign-ups.** Among people active twice in two weeks, 40 %+ "very disappointed" to lose it ([Ellis, via Superhuman](https://review.firstround.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/)). About 40 answers is directional: ±15 points.
- **Weekly growth** ([Graham](https://paulgraham.com/growth.html)): 5–7 % is good, 10 % exceptional, 1 % means not figured out.
- **A launch spike is not validation:** [400+ Product Hunt sign-ups, one payer](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/400-signups-from-product-hunt-1-paying-customer-what-4-days-taught-me-about-launch-vs-traction-7c0aacf745) (one case).

## What changed by 2026
- **AI assistants** send under 0.3 % of web traffic ([Ahrefs, 2026-03](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/)). Earned mentions drive them, and recommendations [vary run to run](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/). Not a first-100 channel. Our own record: 90,568 AI-bot requests, 0 referrals. MCP directories are cheap hygiene; no install data exists.
- **Agent checkout isn't real yet:** [OpenAI retreated from Instant Checkout](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/24/openai-revamps-shopping-experience-in-chatgpt-after-instant-checkout.html) in March 2026.
- **Short video:** accounts under 5K followers averaged 15,160 views on YouTube Shorts against 625 on Reels and 350 on TikTok ([Socialinsider, 2026-11](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-reels-vs-youtube-shorts/)). Test through creators too, not only your own account.
- **Outreach works only when small and human:** 21–50 recipients 6.2 % replies against 2.4 % at 500+, and 69 % of US decision makers dislike AI-written email ([Hunter, 2025](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)).
